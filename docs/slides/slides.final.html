What to do with this

A cheap pre-deployment probe, and four lessons for swarm oversight

01

Test with the pressure on. 0 of 32 swarms broke the rule when the task was solvable.

02

Plant a post, place a canary. Fixed actions make it causal; it separates susceptible models from robust ones.

03

Answer the escalation line. An agent asking a human for an exception is the moment to intervene.

04

Watch the gateway, not the words. Tripwires were perfect; word and board monitors miss quiet agents.

Limitations: simulated sandbox, 12 rounds, 6 seeds per cell, scripted probe with one wording per arm8 / 9
